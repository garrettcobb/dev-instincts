**MZ-L08.** With "Lock reconciled transactions" on, voiding the unreconciled leg of a transfer through `PATCH /transactions/:id/status` or `PATCH /transactions/:id` sets the other account's RECONCILED leg to VOID (reconciled date kept) and moves that account's balance. The lock is asked of the requested row only; `applyVoidTransitionToMirrorLeg` then writes the counterpart without asking. The paired delete (`removeTransfer`) and the bulk route check both legs and refuse. Same cause on the split side: replacing or removing a split parent's lines checks the parent and then deletes the transfer counterpart legs in other accounts whatever their status. Fix: assert the lock over every locked row the write reaches (the mirror leg, the split legs) before writing. Instincts 6, 27.

**MZ-L09.** `updateStatus` accepts VOID -> RECONCILED (the `reconcile` route refuses it), and the mirror helper copies the exact new status onto the counterpart, so the other account's leg becomes RECONCILED with no reconciled date and was never reconciled there. INV-TRANSFER-001 says only the VOID crossing is shared. Fix: refuse VOID -> RECONCILED on the status route; write the counterpart as UNRECONCILED when leaving VOID. Instincts 6, 23.

**MZ-L10.** Category delete refuses only for subcategories and for transactions, splits, scheduled transactions and scheduled splits; payroll deduction rules naming the category are cascaded away, budget lines keep their amount with a null category (main adds `loan_fees`, also cascading). Reassign, the step the refusal points to, moves none of them. Fix: count them in the guard or move them in reassign. Instincts 6, 58.

**MZ-L11.** Reassign selects every transaction id of the user and binds the list as `IN (:...ids)`; TypeORM expands one parameter per id and PostgreSQL's Bind message holds the count in 16 bits, so over 65,535 transactions the request fails and the category can be neither reassigned nor deleted. Fix: a subquery on `transactions.user_id`. Instinct 13.

**MZ-L12.** "Twice a Month" steps 1st -> 31st -> 15th -> 28th; a bill on the 1st and 16th gets the wrong due and auto-post days. Backend and frontend agree and tests assert it; the loan label spells out "15th & month end", the scheduled label does not. Held back; Garrett decides. Instincts 58, 23.
