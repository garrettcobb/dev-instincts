# Rules: the record

Append-only. An entry is never edited, shortened or renumbered. A rule's later change, its measured effect, or its leaving the protocol is a new entry at the end, numbered on, that names the entry it concerns (`PRINCIPLES.md` §3).

**Entries R1 to R375 are the inventory as of 2026-11-02**, the tree at 062cf7e: every rule then in force in the files Garrett named, one entry each. Nothing is judged or removed here. The files: `testing/MINING.md`, `testing/FILING.md`, `testing/CONTROL.md`, `WORKFLOW.md`, `SYNC.md`, the three agent files (`agent/dev-architect.md`, `agent/instinct-reviewer.md`, `agent/senior-coder.md`) and the templates (`templates/*.md`).

How each column was filled:
- **Rule:** the rule in short, plain words. The file's own words are the authority.
- **Where:** the section or item in that file. A section sign (§) in *Where* is that file's own; in *Origin* it names its file.
- **Added:** the date and commit of the first version of the file that holds the rule's present wording: `git log --follow --reverse -S'<a phrase of the rule>'` on the file. A rule reworded since may be older than its date.
- **Origin:** the logged failure the rule answers (a ledger id, an RTIRR entry, a review report, a `LESSONS-LEARNED.md` section, a `metrics/CONVERGENCE.md` row, or a commit that names one), or the measure it enables; otherwise "none recorded". A decision of Garrett's with neither on record is "none recorded".
- **Effect:** "not yet measured", unless a dated number in `metrics/`, the ledger (`ledger/STATS.md`) or a score report bears on the rule. Then the number and where it is, marked "no control" where it is a before and after.

**Not in this inventory:** rules also live in `PRINCIPLES.md`, `DEV-INSTINCTS.md` and `proposals/`, `ledger/README.md`, `agent/INSTALL.md`, `agent/senior-coder-checklist.md`, `testing/TEST-BATTERY.md`, `testing/REVIEW-METHOD.md`, `testing/PROBE-RECIPES.md`, `testing/OFFLINE-ISOLATION.md`, `metrics/CONVERGENCE.md` (its decision rule), `metrics/FILED.md`, `metrics/CONTROL.md`, `README.md`, `LESSONS-LEARNED.md` (*Carry forward*), the stop rules in the plans' handoffs under `reviews/`, and the checks built into `tools/` and `agent/sync-agents.ps1`. A later entry can add them.
